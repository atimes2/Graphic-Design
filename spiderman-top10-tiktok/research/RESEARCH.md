# Research: what makes a top-10 TikTok go viral (Oct 2026)

Research done 25 Oct 2026 for the "Top 10 Most Powerful Spider-Men" video. Most public retention numbers come from creator-tool vendors, so treat them as rough guides, not platform rules. TikTok doesn't publish its ranking formula.

## Findings

| Topic | What the sources say | How it's used in this video |
|---|---|---|
| **The first 3 seconds** | Most sources say the first 3 seconds decide whether a video gets wider reach. Reported targets range from 70% to over 85% of viewers still watching at 3s. The first frame should make the topic clear even with the sound off. | Frame 1 is already "TOP 10 / MOST POWERFUL / SPIDER-MEN EVER", over a flashing montage of the cards. There's no intro and no fade-in. |
| **Hook patterns** | Open loops, bold or contrarian claims, and identity hooks scored highest in one test of 30 hook types. | The hook opens a loop: "Number one can rewrite reality", with #1 shown as a hidden silhouette and "???". |
| **Completion rate** | Watching to the end is described as one of the strongest interest signals, per TikTok Newsroom as quoted by vendors. | The countdown tracker at the top shows 10→1 with #1 locked as "?". Viewers have to stay to the end to see it. |
| **Length** | 30–60s is the most common length and gets strong interaction. One sample put the median length of top-10% videos at about 41s. Lists and quick how-tos work at 20–45s, storytelling at 45–120s. | 53.8s total. All pauses are cut down to about 0.16s, so it averages about 3 words per second. |
| **Captions** | 80% of viral-tier clips used burned-in captions and 79% animated them, in a 13.5M-clip vendor study. Captions are linked to a 12–40% increase in watch time. | Word-by-word captions, 1–3 words at a time, in Montserrat Black with a heavy stroke. The current word turns yellow or the card's color and pops. |
| **Pacing** | Aim for a new visual every 1–2 seconds. | Every card has these timed beats: whip-in, number slam, name pop, sub-title fade, tags slide in, power bar fills, caption changes. |
| **AI voiceover** | Works fine for informational formats like top-10s, where the voice isn't the main draw. | Neural TTS (en-US-GuyNeural, +18% rate, −4Hz pitch) with the pauses cut out. |
| **Comment bait** | Comments and debate drive distribution. Rankings are naturally debatable. | Miles is placed at #8 on purpose, with "Only number eight? Fight me in the comments" and a fake comment card. The video ends with "Who's YOUR #1? Comment below". |
| **Rewatch / loop** | Videos that loop cleanly get extra watch time. | The last card is the 10-card grid and the first frame is the montage, with no fade between them, so the restart feels continuous. |
